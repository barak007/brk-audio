BRK - Palmas

Put the clap where the groove wants it.

Find the claps that lift your track, from a plain backbeat to house, dembow or flamenco palmas. Shape two clap sounds, from a drum machine to a room full of hands, try new patterns until one sticks, then keep it playing the same way on every pass. Use the built-in sounds or send the rhythm to another instrument.

Download for macOS

Or install from Terminal

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/palmas-v0.1.0/install.sh | bash
What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/palmas-v0.1.0/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh

Changelog

0.1.02026-11-25Latest
  • A long SysEx message from the host no longer allocates memory on the audio thread; it was ignored before and still is.
  • The first claps no longer allocate memory while audio plays.
  • First version: clap patterns from a seed in two sounds (A, B or both per clap), eleven engines behind a burst train of hands and a tail, clap styles (backbeat to palmas), flams, pickups, rolls, swells, fills and builds.
  • Copy and paste rows, bars and whole cycles from the right-click menus.
  • Twenty-four factory presets in five groups: Classic (drum-machine, gated, house, disco and arena claps), Organic (real hands, gospel, stomp-and-clap, campfire), Latin (dembow, perreo, clave, flamenco and rumba palmas), Hip-hop & Trap (boom bap, lo-fi, trap, drill, Memphis) and Electronic (UK garage, vowels, Jersey club, footwork, glitch).
