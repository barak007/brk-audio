BRK 3-03

Squelch, slide and accent.

A bass line synth with its own step sequencer, modelled on the circuit of the classic early-80s bass machine. Draw a pattern, then ride Cutoff, Resonance and Envelope while it plays. Twelve factory patterns in Acid, Bass and Lead to start from.

A silver panel with fluted knobs and slide switches, above a green display for the pattern.

What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/brk-303-v0.2.0/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh

Version 0.2.0 / macOS universal / VST3, AU, CLAP / Free early access

  • Seven knobs: Tuning, Cutoff, Resonance, Envelope, Decay, Accent and Volume
  • Saw or pulse wave
  • Accents and slides per step, in a piano roll of up to 64 steps
  • Follows your DAW's tempo and transport, or runs on its own clock
  • Play it from a MIDI keyboard instead of the pattern
  • Tuned against recordings of an original unit, note by note
