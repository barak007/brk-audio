BRK - Tine 73

Shape the tine. Play the response.

A 73-key electric piano that follows your touch, from a soft, glassy tone to a barking attack. Choose the amp it plays through, add drive and tone, and let the stereo tremolo carry the chords. Eight factory sounds to start from, and the sustain pedal works as you would expect.

A black leather case with knobs on the name rail. Lift the lid to see the tines and pickups at work.

What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/tine73-v0.5.0/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh

Version 0.5.0 / macOS universal / VST3, AU, CLAP / Free early access

  • Play softly for a round, glassy tone, harder for the bark
  • Choose the circuit: direct, stage, a suitcase amp or the fuller 80 W suitcase
  • Drive and tone on the name rail, for a clean or gritty sound
  • Stereo tremolo that swings between the speakers, with depth, speed and spread
  • Open the lid to set the hammers, pickups and dampers in the Lab
  • Sustain pedal with half-pedal, and a keyboard you can click
