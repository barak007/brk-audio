BRK - Good Kick

Make the kick you hear in your head.

Draw the punch, pitch drop and tail until the kick fits your track. Add a little noise for a sharper attack or a rougher edge, then play it from your keyboard or drag it into your session as audio.

Download for macOS

Or install from Terminal

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/good-kick-v1.3.1/install.sh | bash
What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/good-kick-v1.3.1/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh

Changelog

1.3.12026-11-11Latest
  • Remove the help panel’s X button; press HELP again to close it.
  • Keep hovered control help visible across timer refreshes; unchanged keyboard focus no longer replaces it.
  • Seal AU, VST3 and CLAP bundles with complete ad-hoc signatures during packaging.
  • Rename the plugin to BRK - Good Kick in hosts and installed bundles; keep plugin IDs, saved host sessions and the existing preset folder.
  • Unify the header: 16 px title, presets beside it, right-aligned version/build details, shared neutral colours and square controls.
  • Apply typed numeric values with Enter; Escape, focus loss and clicks outside cancel entry.
1.3.02026-11-10
  • The help button now opens a contextual footer; a website/PDF manual covers controls and drawing gestures.
  • Report the possible four-second hit tail to hosts.
  • VoiceOver names and reads the header's boxes, the strip cells, the pitch view's Low and High, the preset bar, the tabs and the Shape menu button, and presses them; Tab reaches them, the arrow keys move a focused box or cell as the wheel does (Shift finer) and Return opens a box for typing. The mouse works as before; the envelope editor is still mouse-only.
  • The header's boxes (Length, End phase, Attack, Release, Velocity, Output) and the strip cells under the envelope tabs (Curves, the noise and shape settings) drag evenly when Shift is pressed or let go mid-drag, scroll as far as a trackpad moves instead of racing, and record a whole scroll as one undo step. Hosts take a typed note or Hz for Noise Cutoff and Shape Top too (E5, D#6 -20, 2.5k).
  • The pitch view's Low and High boxes drag and scroll the same way, in whole semitones (Shift drags slower); a typed note or Hz still sets any pitch.
  • The text is a little cooler white, the same as the other plugins on the shared cell look.
  • Drawing an envelope marks the project changed, so VST3 and CLAP hosts offer to save it; before, closing the project after only drawing lost the drawing without asking.
1.2.02026-11-05
  • Presets and sessions save each choice (Shape family, Curves, noise Type, Filter and Mix) by a stable name beside its position, and load it by that name, so they keep their settings when a list gains entries in a later version. Presets and sessions from earlier versions load as before.
  • Hosts can no longer automate the choices (Shape family, Curves, noise Type, Filter and Mix): a host stores a choice as its place in the list, which a new entry would move. Automation already recorded on them is dropped. The knobs (lengths, levels, cutoff, the families' settings and the rest) stay automatable.
1.1.02026-11-01
  • A new look, matching Feedline: flat cells edge to edge, split by thin lines, with no rounded corners or gaps. The title bar holds the name, presets, tempo and help; the settings sit in one strip under it; the envelope tabs are coloured as their curves, so they double as the legend.
  • The Shape view has its own strip under the tabs, like Noise: the family's setting, then three new settings for every family. Depth scales the Shape envelope, Mix blends the plain sine back under the shaped wave (parallel drive), and Top fades the overtones out above a frequency while the fundamental always plays. At their defaults (100 %, 100 %, 22 kHz) kicks sound exactly as before, and older sessions and presets load them there.
  • The zoom readout sits after Curves and reads ×1 for the whole kick instead of "whole kick".
  • Play sounds as the button goes down, not when it is released.
  • Curves chooses how the envelopes' points become lines: Exponential (as before, segments can meet at corners) or Smooth, one line through the points with no corners and no overshoot. Older sessions and presets keep Exponential.
  • The pitch envelope reaches 16 kHz instead of 8 kHz.
  • Times read as DAW note values (1/16, 1/16 D, 1/16 T, 1 bar) from 1/512 to four bars, instead of a list that included quintuplets, septuplets and values down to 1/2048 (1/3584, 2/7, 16/5).
  • Times also accept 1/16 D, 1/16 T and 2 bars when typed.
  • Presets: your presets can live in folders (submenus in the list; type Folder/Name when saving), Rename is in the preset menu, saving over another preset asks first, a preset file dropped on the preset bar loads, and a preset made with a newer version is marked newer. Loading a preset sets each control once, straight to its value.
