BRK - Sonoscopeia

See what your low end is doing.

If the kick and bass are hard to untangle by ear, take a closer look. Watch their notes and pitch bends trace across the screen, see where they overlap, and check the rest of the track without changing what you hear.

Download for macOS

Or install from Terminal

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/sonoscopeia-v1.0.1/install.sh | bash
What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/sonoscopeia-v1.0.1/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh
