BRK - Zero Clip

Push the level. Shape the weight and bite.

Get a little more level from your drums, bass or mix. Tame the peaks and shape the low end separately from the highs, so you can find the right balance of weight, bite and control.

Download for macOS

Or install from Terminal

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/zero-clip-v1.2.1/install.sh | bash
What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/zero-clip-v1.2.1/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh

Changelog

1.2.12026-11-11Latest
  • Remove the help panel’s X button; press HELP again to close it.
  • Keep hovered control help visible across timer refreshes; unchanged keyboard focus no longer replaces it.
  • Seal AU, VST3 and CLAP bundles with complete ad-hoc signatures during packaging.
  • Rename the plugin to BRK - Zero Clip in hosts and installed bundles; keep plugin IDs, saved host sessions and the existing preset folder.
  • Unify the header: 16 px title, presets beside it, right-aligned version/build details, shared neutral colours and square controls.
  • Apply typed numeric values with Enter; Escape, focus loss and clicks outside cancel entry.
1.2.02026-11-10
  • Added the shared preset bar and four factory starting points, with user preset files and named-preset session recall. Output, audition and view settings stay unchanged when loading presets.
  • Added toggleable contextual HELP and a website/PDF parameter manual.
  • VoiceOver names the knobs and reads the Half/Full, slope, phase, oversampling and target tiles, Solo, Bypass, Delta, Match and the link lock as controls it can press; Tab reaches them, the arrow keys move a focused knob and Return presses a focused tile. The mouse works as before; the meters' readouts are not read yet.
1.1.02026-11-05
  • Choices (Oversample, crossover slope and phase, the loudness target, each band's Half/Full) are saved in sessions by stable IDs beside their positions, so a session keeps its settings when a list changes, such as a new loudness target landing mid-list. Sessions saved before load as they did.
  • Choices can no longer be automated by hosts (a host stores a choice's place in its list, which moves when the list changes), so automation already recorded on them is dropped. The on/off switches (Link, Bypass, Delta, Gain match, Solo) were already toggles and can still be automated.
1.0.02026-11-04
  • First version: a two-band clipper whose gain only changes at zero crossings. A crossover splits low and high; in both bands every lobe is measured whole and played with one gain, under the band's threshold: a half cycle, or a whole cycle in Full mode (per band). Threshold per band, linked by a lock by default, and Solo per band. Whole cycles down to 15 Hz, at a fixed 70 ms latency. Input and Output drive only the clipped path, so Mix at 0 bypasses them too.
  • Transient and Smooth per band: give back a share of each attack, measured on the entire signal as how far it rises over its smoothed level before it, as less reduction on the lobe, at most the threshold's distance from 0 dB; the body and steady sounds are clipped as before. Kept attacks show blue.
  • A two-lane display of both bands (the louder channel at each moment): each lobe as it came in and as it leaves, red lines to drag for each band's threshold, gain reduction, and the crossings where the gain changes, triggered on a rising crossing, with a View slider from 5 ms to 2 s and click to freeze; content below 15 Hz is marked. Half, Full and Solo carry icons.
  • Gain reduction read two ways, picked by Env and Actual in the top meter's header and kept with the session: Env is the gain each lobe is given, Actual (the default) what that takes off the audio, measured at the threshold, so a Release held over a quiet sound or silence reads as little as it changes. The meters, their readouts and the lanes' bars follow it.
  • The controls sit between the lanes as a signal chain, Input to Output, with dots flowing along it while sound passes, and the threshold lock on a rail joining the two lanes' red lines.
  • Refinements in the chain, each off at zero: per band Knee and Glide (Attack, Release, letting the gain glide across lobes, far less intermodulation), and between the strips Link (Band sum keeps the bands' sum under the threshold, Stereo link) and Oversample 1x, 2x, 4x or 8x (far less aliasing), at the same 70 ms latency; switching crossfades.
  • Crossover slope of 12, 24 or 48 dB/oct, each summing back flat; changing it crossfades.
  • Crossover phase: Natural, or Linear (no phase turned: the bands add back to the input itself, at 40 ms more latency).
  • Bypass, latency-compensated and faded, also what the host's bypass switches.
  • Meters in the header: input and output loudness (BS.1770 momentary and short-term) and true peak, coloured against a loudness target as K-meter zones, the loudness gained in LU and the peak-to-loudness ratio before and after; Delta (hear only what the clipping changes) and Match (compare at the same loudness).
  • View is a small knob in the chain, from 5 ms to 2 s, 2 s by default, kept with the session.
  • A 3D gain reduction meter beside each lane: the newest 4 ms at the front with no smoothing, the last second receding behind it, so short reductions leave a visible tail.
