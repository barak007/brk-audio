Gear Comp

Help the parts feel like they belong together.

Pull a drum bus together, settle a lively track or add a bit more punch. Shape how the compression catches and lets go, then blend it with the original until the movement feels right.

A familiar studio compressor layout, with a brushed-metal face, a needle meter and clear controls.

What this does

Downloads the release archive from GitHub, checks it against the checksum fixed at release time and copies the chosen formats into ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. Installs in your own plugin folders without sudo, refreshes the Audio Unit list when AU is selected, and replaces each installed format only once its new bundle is fully copied.

To read the script first, download it and open it in TextEdit (this runs nothing):

curl -fsSL https://github.com/barak007/brk-audio/releases/download/gear-comp-v0.1.1/install.sh -o install.sh && open -e install.sh

Version 0.1.1 / macOS universal / VST3, AU, CLAP / Free early access

  • Shape how firmly the compressor grabs and how quickly it lets go
  • A release that adapts to the music, from short hits to sustained phrases
  • Use the sidechain filter to make the compression less sensitive to deep kicks and bass
  • Choose a gentler squeeze or a firmer grip with the Knee switch
  • Blend in compression with Mix to keep some of the original feel
  • Arm Auto Gain before a change to help match the listening level
  • Start with a preset and watch the needle as you listen

Changelog

0.1.12026-11-11Latest
  • Remove the help panel’s X button; press HELP again to close it.
  • Seal AU, VST3 and CLAP bundles with complete ad-hoc signatures during packaging.
  • Shared numeric cells now apply typed values only with Enter; Escape, focus loss and outside clicks cancel entry.
0.1.02026-11-10
  • Auto Gain now arms idle and learns only from direct user sound-knob edits, never automation, switches, presets or session recall. Presets exclude Auto Gain; offline rendering holds the saved Output.
  • Keep every header action visible at the minimum size and preserve width when toggling HELP.
  • Rendered controls and Auto Gain support named keyboard/VoiceOver interaction; HELP links to the website/PDF manual.
  • First version: a compressor in a rendered rack unit, in the look of Gear Tube's amplifier: Threshold, Ratio, Attack, Release, Mix and Output, a VU meter of gain reduction or output, four gain reduction lamps (1, 2, 4 and 8 dB), and switches for GR / OUT, the Sidechain (Off, 60 Hz, 120 Hz) and the Knee (Hard, Soft, Wide) on the meter's black field, and Power at the right end.
  • The detector is built as an analog compressor's is (a peak follower with an RMS floor behind a coupling high-pass, then the soft-knee gain computer), to reduce low-frequency gain modulation; the release follows the program (about three times faster after a short burst, the full time after sustained compression).
  • A help bar (HELP, closed to begin with) says what each control, lamp and the meter is.
  • Thirteen factory presets (Glue, Tracking, Drums, Parallel), level-matched by Auto Gain.
  • Power crossfades over 5 ms instead of clicking, and knobs no longer jump when Shift is pressed mid-drag.
