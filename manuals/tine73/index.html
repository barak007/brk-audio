BRK - Tine 73

User manual and parameter reference · 0.5.1

A 73-note electric piano with a textured Stage-style instrument, an opening Lab, mechanical action, identified harmonic response, output circuits and optical stereo tremolo.

Quick start

  1. Insert Tine 73 on an instrument track and play MIDI notes E1–E7 (28–100), or click the keyboard.
  2. Load a factory sound from the preset menu, or begin with Init. Adjust Touch to suit your MIDI controller.
  3. Turn Tone, Drive and Volume on the rendered name rail, and the tremolo's Depth and Speed beside them. Raise Spread for stereo motion; open Lab for Touch, Hammer, pickup and electronics controls.

Editor window

The editor is 1040 by 514 pixels. CLOSE-UP (bottom left) moves the view nearer the name rail; LAB (bottom right) lifts the cover to reveal the harp and opens its controls beside the instrument. HELP adds a footer below. The current instance remembers the view; layout is not part of sound presets or saved sessions.

Operation

Lab / Open lifts the cover and reveals the harp. Action, pickup, damper and electronics controls appear in a side panel. Lab / Close changes only the view. A key goes down while it is held, from the pointer or MIDI. The plugin draws Blender renders; no live 3D renderer runs during playback.

The name rail holds the playing controls: Circuit, Tone, Drive and Volume, the tremolo's Depth, Speed and Spread, and the Vintage/Balanced switch. Close-up moves the view nearer and lower, so the rail's legends read.

Vintage swings over the whole optical range, down to near silence at full Depth, with the lamp's rounded shoulders, and keeps the average level steady across Depth and Rate. Its breathing is asymmetric, so allow extra peak headroom, up to about 4.3 dB with full-depth Sine; the preview labels its 0–2 gain scale. Balanced normalizes the swing and keeps combined L/R power steady at full Spread. Intermediate Spread still moves both channels, Spread 0 is mono, and Depth 0 is off. Both are designed responses, not measured hardware calibration.

Studio combines modal mechanics with numerical harmonic/decay parameters identified from recorded Stage 73 notes. It does not play recorded samples. Original is the simpler tine and pickup model: rounder, with its bark from the pickup geometry. Both play through the same circuits, tremolo and output. Model applies to the next strike, and a ringing note stays in the model that struck it. Version-one sessions and presets select Original automatically.

Each tine retains vibration across re-strikes. The hammer has material-dependent stiffness, damped contact and a calibrated excitation response. Touch adjusts the MIDI velocity curve. Character supplies stable tuning/decay differences.

Both models respond to velocity through Touch. Stroke variation adds a balanced eight-strike cycle of small hammer strength, hardness and approach differences in either model. Each key/channel advances separately; reset restarts the cycle. Zero turns it off. No recorded samples rotate and tuning is not randomized.

CC64 controls damper lift continuously. Pitch bend is ±2 semitones per channel. CC123 releases keys while respecting the pedal, CC120 kills that channel’s tines, and CC121 resets pedal and bend.

Distance changes pickup operating strength and level; Offset changes even/odd harmonic balance. Bell changes metallic attack and upper harmonic presence. Decay scales identified fast and long-lived components; Damper sets the release response.

Direct at Drive 0 stays clean. Stage models the passive bass-control network; Tone acts as its bass-cut setting. Suitcase adds a reduced transistor preamp. Suitcase 80 follows the 80 W preamp part by part: its input stage, its Treble and Bass controls (Tone, and Bass in Lab) and its 5 kHz output filter; the other circuits ignore Bass. Drive changes output-stage excitation; in Direct/Suitcase, Tone balances body and treble. Cabinet blends an analytical speaker response.

Lab → Anti-alias → Integrated refines the transistor cutoff solve and averages its transfer over each internal sample, followed by output coupling. Classic preserves the prior drive behavior. Both use 4x oversampling. Integrated changes driven tone slightly and reduces measured output-stage aliasing; it does not establish an alias-free complete instrument.

Depth, Rate and Spread control free-running tremolo. Bulb uses thermal and light-dependent recovery, LED responds faster, and Sine is sinusoidal.

Output is final gain; Direct is not silently limited. Bypass fades to silence while the mechanism and MIDI state keep updating. Processing uses 4x oversampling, reports its latency, and allows a maximum 120-second autonomous tail.

In performance/Lab cells, drag/scroll, Shift for fine changes, double-click to type and Alt-click for default. The rendered rail knobs use double-click for default; Lab and the tremolo panel take typed values. Tab and arrows operate parameter controls. HELP describes the hovered/focused control; Manual / PDF opens the included offline manual.

The recorded reference has EQ, denoising and adjusted levels; physical strike speeds are unknown. Direct, Stage, Suitcase and the optical time constants are reduced models; Suitcase 80 follows the 80 W preamp's drawing with assumed transistor gain and harp level, ideal followers and no power amplifier; the cabinet is analytical. Cross-key mechanical coupling, recorded room/action noises and a complete measured vintage circuit/cabinet reconstruction remain outside this build.

Presets and files

The preset bar provides eight factory sounds, including Clean Suitcase with Integrated anti-aliasing, Balanced Suitcase with Balanced tremolo, Living Suitcase with 35% Stroke variation and Suitcase 80 through the 80 W preamp, and saves user presets with the .tine73 extension. User files live under ~/Library/Audio/Presets/Barakigal Audio/BRK - Tine 73.

The host session recalls every parameter and preset selection. Sound presets leave Bypass unchanged. No samples need relinking.

Installation and help

Download the macOS archive from the product page and quit your audio host. Place the AU .component in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, VST3 in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, or CLAP in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP. The product page also offers an installer with format selection. Restart or rescan the host. To uninstall, quit the host and remove that product's bundles from those folders; saved sessions and user presets are separate.

For questions or a problem report, use BRK Audio support on GitHub. Include the plugin version, macOS version, host and format, sample rate and a short description of how to reproduce it.
