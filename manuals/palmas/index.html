BRK - Palmas

User manual and parameter reference · 0.1.0

Build clap patterns from a seed, from a plain backbeat to dembow, house or flamenco palmas, and play them through two clap sounds, A and B. Palmas plays its own clap synthesis and can send the rhythm as MIDI.

Quick start

  1. Insert Palmas on an instrument track, leave Trigger on Note, and hold a MIDI note or press PLAY.
  2. Load a factory preset, or choose A–D for the four starting patterns. Use NEW for another seed, MUTATE for a smaller change, and Ghosts, Stack and Split to decide how many claps play and which sound plays them.
  3. Select CLAP A or CLAP B to shape that sound, or click a step to edit it. Save your project or a user preset when you find a take to keep.

Editor window

The editor is 1000 by 722 pixels with HELP closed. Opening HELP adds a footer below without moving the existing controls; its Manual / PDF action opens this guide. Shared cells, buttons and the preset bar support keyboard and VoiceOver interaction; drawing and dragging in the step grid require a mouse. Press HELP again to close the help area.

Operation

Note trigger plays while a note is held; Transport trigger follows the host transport. PLAY auditions without a note. The pattern follows the song position, so looping, scrubbing or offline rendering reproduces the same take.

MIDI notes 36–39, labelled C1–D#1 in Palmas, select slots A–D and open the note gate while held. Other notes play the selected slot. Note velocity changes level, and the modulation wheel raises Ghosts.

Each slot has its own seed, style, grid, layer split, moves and fills. The slots share the cycle length, groove and both clap sounds. A cycle can end between bar lines; it repeats at its own length and can drift across the host's bars.

NEW, MUTATE and DICE have a local history reached with the < and > buttons. A grid paste cannot be undone; clipboard contents and the local take history are not saved with the project.

MIDI Out sends the claps on channel 10: A as note 39 (D#1, the General MIDI hand clap) and B as note 40 (E1). A flam's grace is a note of its own; a swell sends none. Host routing support differs by format. Output Off silences the synth and sends only MIDI.

HELP follows the hovered or keyboard-focused control. The parameter table documents host parameters; NEW, MUTATE, the dice buttons, PLAY, clipboard actions and hand-edited steps are described in the operation sections.

Playing it

  • Trigger: Note plays while any note is held, placed by the host’s song position (a note up to 30 ms late still plays the clap it just missed); with the transport stopped, from the first step at the note. Transport plays whenever the host plays. PLAY plays without a note.
  • C1 to D#1 pick slots A to D, and also open the gate; the last picked stays picked. Note velocity 100 and up plays the claps as they are, softer notes softer. The mod wheel takes Ghosts up toward every step.
  • MIDI Out sends the claps on channel 10: A as D#1 (39, the General MIDI hand clap), B as E1 (40). A flam’s grace is a note of its own; a swell sends none.

The pattern

Four slots, A to D, each its own pattern. They start as a backbeat, a trap half-time (pickups, rolls, a build, swells), a dembow and palmas.

A clap pattern is built round its anchors, which always play: the backbeat, or the style’s own places. Ghosts come on top, each step scored by the style’s weight for its place blended with the seed’s number by Complexity, and taken from the top: turning Ghosts up only adds claps, turning it down only takes the same ones away.

PATTERN, per slot:

  • Style: Backbeat, House, 2-Step and Gospel anchor on 2 and 4 (their ghosts lean to the off eighths, the sixteenths round the beat, or the double clap before it); Half-time on 3; Dembow on its 3-3-2; Clave and Rumba on the son and rumba claves, 3-2 over two bars; Palmas on the twelve-beat compás of the soleá and the bulería, a beat to an eighth note, accents on 3, 6, 8, 10 and 12, counted from the cycle’s start across the bar lines (a 3-bar cycle holds two); Footwork on its broken sixteenths; Euclid spreads Ghosts’ share of the steps as evenly as they go, turned by the seed.
  • Grid: 1/8, 1/8 T, 1/16 or 1/16 T.
  • Seed, NEW, Mutation, MUTATE, Complexity, Evolve (how much the bars differ) and Accent (how much softer the ghosts and pickups are than the anchors).

LAYERS: each clap plays on A, on B or on both.

  • Stack: how many play on both, the strongest first: the anchors, then the loudest ghosts.
  • Split: of the claps not stacked, the share B plays. The anchors stay on A. At Split 100 and Stack 0 the palmas slot is claras on A and sordas on B.

MOVES, the things claps do:

  • Pickups: the chance of a clap on the step just before each anchor.
  • Flams and Flam Gap: a softer grace clap a hair before its clap, likelier on the anchors.
  • Rolls and Roll Hits: ghosts and pickups split into 2 up to Roll Hits claps, rising into the last.
  • Build: the cycle’s last bar fills toward its end, louder and rolling faster as it goes.
  • Swells and Swell Length: a reverse swell rising out of nothing into an anchor, over that many steps (each sound’s Swell Level sets how loud).

FILLS, the tweaks at the end of a phrase while the rest stays steady:

  • Fills: how much a fill adds: claps where there were none, denser, louder and likelier to roll and flam the nearer the end. Turned up, it only adds.
  • Every: how often: at the end of every bar, every 2, 3 or 4 bars (counted from the cycle’s start; the cycle’s end always ends one), or once, at the end of the cycle.
  • Length: how far before the end of its span the fill starts, in beats (1/2 to 8).
  • Build: the cycle’s last bar fills toward its end, louder and rolling faster as it goes.

GROOVE (shared): Swing (-100 to +100%), Swing On (Steps or 1/8), Humanize (late and softer, A and B apart). Cycle (shared): whole bars and eighth notes over them, up to 8 bars.

DICE in each section’s title rolls it, DICE ALL all of them and both sounds, as far as Range says; < and > step through a slot’s NEW, MUTATE and DICE. A dice never moves the seed, Mutation, Grid, a sound’s Level, Pan or Nudge, or the output.

The grid

One bar of the shown slot, a row for each clap. Each clap is drawn as it sounds: a tick for each hand where it lands, its ring fading after the last hand, a flam’s grace before it, a swell’s wedge rising into it, a roll split into its claps. The faint steps are the next ones Ghosts would bring in. A dot marks a step set by hand.

  • Click a step to select it (the bar holds; FOLLOW or Esc lets go); the STEP row shows and sets its Velocity, Claps (its roll), Flam, Swell, Decay and Pan. Double-click a value to give it back; RESET gives back all.
  • Double-click a step to add a clap or take it away; drag a clap up or down for velocity, sideways for its roll.
  • Click a row’s name to switch that sound off (silent, no notes) or on.
  • Right-click a row (its name or its steps) to copy it, every bar; to paste a copied row over it or add its claps; or to clear its edits. Any row onto any row. Right-click a bar in the strip to copy it (both rows), paste or add a bar, or clear it; or to copy the whole cycle (the slot’s settings and its steps set by hand) and paste it over another slot, which then plays the same, or add only its claps. A row or a bar goes only onto a slot on the grid it was copied on. Copied steps are taken as they play and pasted as set by hand; one thing at a time, kept while the plugin is open.
  • CLEAR gives back every step of the slot set by hand. Steps set by hand are saved with the session and presets.
  • The strip under the grid shows every bar of the cycle; click one to hold it.

The sound

A clap is a few hands landing a few milliseconds apart, so every sound is built the same way: the engines make one hand, the burst train lays the hands out, then Tone, Drive, the sound’s Level and place, and its tail. CLAP A and CLAP B pick which sound shows; COPY TO copies it to the other (not its Level, Pan or Nudge).

  • Engine A, Engine B, Morph (equal power), Color (each engine’s own), Pitch (±12 st), Tone, Decay (the last hand’s ring; the hands before it ring until the next lands) and Drive (tanh, antialiased).
    • Analog: band-passed noise, the drum machine’s clap.
    • Palms: a hand’s air pocket, a burst into a resonance: cupped (Color 0) low and ringing, flat (100) high and short, each hand its own by Vary.
    • Crowd: three clappers to a hand, each its own hands, loose by Color.
    • Snap: a finger snap: a click, a ringing crack and the thump of the palm.
    • Slap: a body slap, chest (Color 0) to palm.
    • Comb: a hollow, tuned tube; Color sets how long it rings, Pitch its note.
    • Formant: noise through a vowel’s formants; Color moves through u, o, a, e, i.
    • FM: a ringing, tonal clap.
    • Zap: a fast falling sine and a click, electro.
    • Grain: a cloud of tiny grains; Color sets how many.
    • Bit: a lo-fi sampler’s clap; Color crushes it.
  • The burst train: Hands (1 to 12), Spread (2 to 40 ms between them), Shape (the gaps closing up toward the last hand, or opening out), Slope (the first or the last hand loudest), Scatter (each hand to its own side, panning only), Vary (each hand’s timing, level and tune), and Nudge (the sound early or late, ±30 ms, against the beat and the other sound).
  • Tail: Room, Plate, Gated (a big room cut after Tail Length) or Reverse (a room swelling up to Tail Length and stopping), with Tail Level, Tail Length, Tail Tone and Pre-delay; Swell Level, how loud its reverse swells are. Then Level and Pan.
  • OUTPUT: Dynamics (how much velocity moves the level), Low Cut (12 dB an octave, to 4 kHz), Output (Off sends only MIDI).

Presets and files

The preset bar offers twenty-four factory presets in five groups: Classic, Organic, Latin, Hip-hop & Trap and Electronic. Each sets both clap sounds and slot A's pattern.

User presets use .palmas files in ~/Library/Audio/Presets/Barakigal Audio/Palmas. Presets and the host session hold the sounds, every slot's pattern and the steps set by hand, with stable choice IDs. Loading a preset leaves Bypass and Dice Range unchanged.

The copy/paste clipboard and the NEW/MUTATE/DICE history exist only while the plugin is open. A paste cannot be undone; save a preset or project before making a change you may want to keep separately.

Installation and help

Download the macOS archive from the product page and quit your audio host. Place the AU .component in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, VST3 in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, or CLAP in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP. The product page also offers an installer with format selection. Restart or rescan the host. To uninstall, quit the host and remove that product's bundles from those folders; saved sessions and user presets are separate.

For questions or a problem report, use BRK Audio support on GitHub. Include the plugin version, macOS version, host and format, sample rate and a short description of how to reproduce it.
